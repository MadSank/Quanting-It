She reveals 32 one-time keys picked by the message’s hash. Bob checks them against quantum copies.

Single photons build a shared secret. Nobody can copy one without changing it.

eve tries to look

Her measurements push Bob’s error rate past 11%. The key is thrown away before it is used.

the sentence is locked

A fresh key seals it with AES-256-GCM. This is all an eavesdropper keeps.

bob reads it

Signature checked, seal opened, sequence number new. Delivered.
